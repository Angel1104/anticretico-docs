# Benchmark

Comparable apps studied in discovery. Approved with the MVP.

| App | What it does well | What it does badly | Copy | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Arrived Homes** (US) | Fractional single-family rentals from USD 100; clean catalog UX with live funding bar and property-by-property state; conservative yield framing. | US-only, SEC Reg A+ overhead, slow to list new properties, no on-chain transparency. | Fractional model with a per-property funding progress bar; clear property state surfaced on every card; yield shown as a historical range, not a promise. | SEC/Reg-A regulatory stack; the "yield prediction" calculators that imply certainty. |
| **RealT** (US / Detroit) | Tokenized rental properties on Ethereum/Gnosis; rent distributed on-chain weekly; public wallet-level accounting. | Ethereum gas friction; US property custody via off-chain LLC with limited legal visibility to foreign holders; secondary market thin. | On-chain economic tokens, public per-transaction explorer link, weekly/monthly distribution pattern surfaced in the UI. | Ethereum L1 rail (gas too high for 1k-USDC tickets); opaque LLC/token legal relationship. |
| **Lofty.ai** (US on Algorand) | Low ticket (USD 50), near-zero chain fees via Algorand, per-property DAO voting on operational decisions. | Still US property + US LLC wrapper; depends on Algorand liquidity; thin disclosure of legal recourse if the LLC fails. | Near-zero-fee chain for distributions (we use Stellar instead), investor dashboard that shows accrued rent in real time, 1k-USDC-tier friendliness. | US property wrapper; DAO-vote complexity for MVP (we leave operational calls to the SRL). |
| **Fundrise** (US) | Mature compliance story and investor onboarding; strong "yield is variable" copy. | Fully off-chain, long redemption windows, opaque underlying assets. | Risk disclosure phrasing around variable yield and lock-up; KYC-before-funding flow. | Off-chain, slow redemption, "fund" abstraction (we show one anticrético per token series). |
| **Bolivian informal anticrético market** (status quo) | Culturally understood; large existing inventory; owners trust it. | Fully cash, fraud risk, 10k–40k USD barrier, no secondary exit, no transparency, owners sometimes spend capital before refund. | Cultural familiarity, use the exact term *anticrético*, map to the existing "cash + Derechos Reales" mental model. | The informal/cash workflow itself: we replace it with on-chain escrow + inscribed lien. |

Our difference, in one sentence: Anticretico is the only platform that fractionalises a **legally inscribed Bolivian anticrético lien** (not a US LLC wrapper) at 1,000 USDC tickets, with Trustless Work escrow protecting the capital until Derechos Reales inscription and Pollar distributing rent on Stellar for near-zero fees.
